# Skill: VisionaryTone

## Purpose
Maintains the "visionary yet accessible" brand voice throughout all content. Ensures writing is inspiring, clear, engaging, and free from jargon or condescension. Every sentence should convey possibility and clarity.

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Rewrite a Bland Introduction
**Before:**
"This chapter covers LLMs. LLMs are neural networks. They process text."

**After (VisionaryTone):**
"Large Language Models (LLMs) are the bridge between human language and robot action. By understanding how they work, you'll unlock a new era of autonomous systems that truly comprehend intent."

### Example 2: Make a Complex Topic Accessible
**Before:**
"Transformer architectures use multi-headed attention mechanisms with positional encoding."

**After (VisionaryTone):**
"Transformers are like translators with multiple perspectives. Each 'perspective' (attention head) focuses on different parts of the input, helping the model understand context from multiple angles—similar to how you might read a robotics paper while considering its practical implications, ethical impact, and connection to prior work."

## Required Constraints

1. **Active voice preferred** — "The robot navigates" not "Navigation is performed"
2. **Second person when teaching** — "You'll learn to..." not "Learners will..."
3. **Concrete examples over abstractions** — Always tie concepts to real robots (Boston Dynamics, Tesla, etc.)
4. **Avoid:** jargon without explanation, passive-aggressive tone, overuse of "simply" or "just"
5. **Incorporate wonder** — Show why this matters: "This is how the future of robotics works."

## Quality Check
- ✅ Does it inspire? (Could a learner get excited?)
- ✅ Is it clear? (Could a non-expert understand it?)
- ✅ Is it honest? (Does it acknowledge limitations?)
