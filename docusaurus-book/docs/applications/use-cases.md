# Real-World Use Cases

Physical AI is already solving real problems. Here are today's leading applications.

## Manufacturing & Assembly

**Current Reality**:
- Robot arms assemble electronics, appliances, and vehicles
- Collaborative robots work safely alongside humans
- Computer vision inspects quality automatically
- Production lines optimize in real-time

**Skills needed**: ROS 2, motion planning, computer vision

## Warehouse & Logistics

**Current Reality**:
- Amazon uses over 500,000 mobile robots for fulfillment
- Robots move inventory autonomously between stations
- AI optimizes paths and delivery sequences
- Reduces human walking by millions of steps daily

**Companies**:
- Amazon Robotics
- Fetch Robotics
- Boston Dynamics

**Skills needed**: ROS 2, navigation, distributed systems

## Healthcare & Surgery

**Current Reality**:
- Robotic surgery provides precision for delicate operations
- Surgical robots reduce tremor and enable remote surgery
- AI assists surgeons with real-time guidance
- Disinfection robots operate in pandemic-response hospitals

**Companies**:
- da Vinci (surgical robotics leader)
- Intuitive Surgical
- Omnicell

**Skills needed**: ROS 2, real-time control, safety systems

## Autonomous Vehicles

**Current Reality**:
- Self-driving cars navigate city streets
- Autonomous delivery vehicles transport packages
- Ride-sharing fleets operate with AI control
- Real-time perception guides every decision

**Companies**:
- Tesla
- Waymo
- Cruise

**Skills needed**: ROS 2, perception, path planning, simulation

## Humanoid Robots (Emerging)

**Future Vision**:
- General-purpose robots perform diverse tasks
- Learn new skills quickly
- Work alongside humans safely
- Could eventually do most human jobs

**Companies**:
- Boston Dynamics (Atlas)
- Tesla (Optimus)
- Figure AI

**Skills needed**: ROS 2, deep learning, manipulation, learning from demonstration

## Research & Exploration

**Current Reality**:
- Quadruped robots explore extreme environments
- Underwater robots map ocean floors
- Space robots conduct planetary research
- Hazardous-site inspection robots work where humans cannot

**Examples**:
- NASA Mars rovers (Curiosity, Perseverance)
- DARPA robotics competitions
- Deep-sea exploration robots

**Skills needed**: ROS 2, control theory, sensor fusion

---

**Key Takeaway**: ROS 2 is the lingua franca across all these applications. Learning it opens doors to any robotics field.
