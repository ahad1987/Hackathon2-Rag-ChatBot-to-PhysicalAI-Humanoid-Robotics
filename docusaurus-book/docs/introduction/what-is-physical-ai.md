# What is Physical AI?

Physical AI combines artificial intelligence with robotics and sensors to create systems that don't just think—they act in the real world.

## Definition

Physical AI is artificial intelligence deployed in systems that can:
- **Sense**: Gather data from cameras, lidar, touch sensors, and more
- **Think**: Process data and make decisions using machine learning and reasoning
- **Act**: Control motors, arms, grippers, and other actuators to interact with the physical world

## Why It Matters

For decades, AI lived in servers and cloud computers. It processed text, images, and data but never touched the physical world.

Physical AI changes this. Robots with AI can now:
- Pick up delicate objects without breaking them
- Navigate unknown environments
- Understand human gestures and respond
- Learn from experience in the real world

## Examples

- **Warehouse robots**: Move packages autonomously through warehouses
- **Medical robots**: Assist surgeons with precision during operations
- **Humanoid robots**: Walk, grasp, and perform tasks like humans
- **Robot arms**: Assemble products with speed and accuracy
- **Autonomous vehicles**: Navigate traffic and understand road conditions

## The Digital Human

Humanoid robots are the frontier of Physical AI—machines shaped like humans that can eventually perform human-like work.
