# Evolution of Systems

Robots didn't start as humanoid machines learning from experience. They evolved through decades of innovation.

## Industrial Robots (1960s-1990s)

Early robots were **fixed, repetitive machines**.

- Bolted to factory floors
- Programmed for one specific task
- Could not adapt to changes
- No sensors; just follow pre-recorded movements

Example: A robot arm that welds the same car door thousands of times.

## Mobile Robots (1990s-2010s)

Robots gained **mobility and basic sensing**.

- Could move from place to place
- Had cameras and basic sensors
- Could avoid obstacles
- Still largely scripted; limited decision-making

Example: Roomba vacuum cleaners that follow programmed patterns.

## AI-Enabled Robots (2010s-Present)

Modern robots now have **learning and reasoning**.

- Real-time decision-making with machine learning
- Adapt to new environments and tasks
- Learn from experience and data
- Collaborate with humans safely

Example: Warehouse robots that optimize paths, avoid humans, and adapt to changing layouts.

## The Humanoid Future

The next frontier is **humanoid robots with general physical intelligence**.

- Learn new tasks with minimal examples
- Manipulate objects with human-like dexterity
- Understand context and make complex decisions
- Work safely alongside humans
